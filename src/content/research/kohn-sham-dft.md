---
title: Kohn–Sham density functional theory
summary: Conventional KS-DFT with careful numerical convergence—pseudopotentials, basis sets, and k-point grids—across chemical space.
order: 2
---

We apply KS-DFT to materials spanning nearly the full periodic table. Direct minimization on the complex Stiefel manifold and related algorithms improve robustness for finite and extended systems. Reliable thermodynamics demands rigorous tests of numerical parameters at each thermodynamic condition.
